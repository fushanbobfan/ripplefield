Ripplefield

A ripple tank in your browser. Click to add a source, drag to draw walls, and watch waves interfere.

Your browser does not support the canvas element.

Shortcuts: Space pause, . step, C calm the water, R reset, V switch view, 1–5 pick a tool.

Amber is a crest, blue a trough. Sand-coloured cells are walls; teal-tinted water is shallow and slow. Waves leaving the tank are absorbed at the edges so the tank behaves as if it were much larger.
